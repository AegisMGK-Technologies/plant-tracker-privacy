Privacy Policy

My Plant Watering Tracker
Developer: AegisMGK Technologies, LLC
Last updated: May 2026

Overview

My Plant Watering Tracker is designed with your privacy as the top priority. All plant care data you enter stays on your device and, if you choose, in your own personal iCloud account. We never see, collect, or have access to any of your data.

Data You Enter

The app stores the following information only on your device or in your personal iCloud account (if you enable iCloud sync):

  • Plant names, species, and personal notes
  • Care schedules (watering, fertilizing, and misting frequencies)
  • Care history logs (dates and notes for each care event)
  • Plant photos you optionally add from your camera or photo library
  • Locations you create to organize your plants (e.g., Living Room, Patio)
  • App preferences (active theme, notification settings)

No account, login credentials, or personal identifying information is ever requested or required to use My Plant Watering Tracker.

Camera and Photo Library

My Plant Watering Tracker can optionally access your camera or photo library if you choose to add a photo to a plant profile. This permission is requested only at the moment you tap the photo option — never on launch. Photos are stored locally on your device or, if iCloud sync is enabled, in your private iCloud account. We do not upload photos to any server or view them in any way.

iCloud Sync (Optional)

If you choose to enable iCloud sync, your data is stored in your personal iCloud private database using Apple's CloudKit. This data is associated with your Apple ID and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. AegisMGK Technologies, LLC cannot access data stored in your iCloud account.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, the app schedules local plant care reminders on your device (e.g., "Time to water your Monstera"). Notification permission is requested only when you enable reminders in the Settings tab — never automatically on first launch. All notifications are generated and delivered entirely on-device. No notification content is sent to any server.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect analytics, crash reports, or usage data
  • We do not use advertising networks or tracking frameworks
  • We do not share any data with third parties
  • We do not operate servers that receive your data
  • We do not require an account or registration to use the app

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a policy change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
